01 / The approach

Build. Test. Learn. Repeat.

A lot of what I’m doing starts with a question: how does this work, could this be easier, or can I build something that does it differently?

Working through those questions is how I learn. Sometimes the result is a useful tool. Sometimes it’s a fix, a better approach, or a clearer idea of what to try next.

Projects with a purpose.

My own projects give me space to experiment with design, code and connected systems. I’m interested in how websites, AI and automation can fit together to solve practical problems.

That carries into client work too. The aim is to understand what someone needs, work out what will help, and build something that makes sense for them — whether that’s a website, an integration or a tool that takes some repetitive work off their hands.

The Projects page shows what I’m working on. The Journal follows the process: progress, problems, fixes and things I’m learning along the way.

More than the finished result.

I want YouTube to be another way to share that process — showing how things come together, talking through ideas and working with other creators.

There’s a community side taking shape too. NoxiVerse is a planned Minecraft community, starting with a fantasy world of castles, quests and shared adventures.

If you’re building something, have an idea you want to explore, or would like to collaborate on a project or video, I’d like to hear about it.
